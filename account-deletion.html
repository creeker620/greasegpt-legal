Delete your GreaseGPT account

This page explains how to permanently delete your GreaseGPT account and all data associated with it.

What gets deleted

  • Your account (email, password hash, Google Sign-In link)
  • All saved vehicles in your Garage (make, model, year, VIN, mileage, notes)
  • All diagnosis conversations and chat history
  • Service & maintenance logs
  • Usage counters and subscription linkage

Deletion is immediate and irreversible. We do not retain backups after 30 days.

Option 1 — Delete inside the app (fastest)

  1. Open the GreaseGPT app on your device
  2. Go to Profile (top-right)
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm

Your account and all associated data are permanently erased within seconds.

Option 2 — Request deletion by email

If you can't sign in to the app, email us and we will permanently delete your account within 7 days (usually within 24 hours). Please send the request from the same email address that's on your GreaseGPT account so we can verify your identity.

Email: greasegpt.app@gmail.com

Subject line: Delete my GreaseGPT account

Body should include:

  • The email address on your GreaseGPT account
  • (Optional) Reason for deletion
Email deletion request

Confirmation

Once your account is deleted, you'll receive a confirmation email. If you re-install the app afterward, you'll need to create a new account — the old data cannot be recovered.

Questions?

Email greasegpt.app@gmail.com and we'll respond within 48 hours.
