# Privacy Policy — GreaseGPT

**Effective date:** August 2, 2026
**Last updated:** August 2, 2026

GreaseGPT ("we", "our", "the app") is an AI-powered automotive diagnostic assistant for mobile devices.
This policy explains what data we collect, why, who we share it with, and how you can delete it.

---

## 1. Data we collect

We collect the following information when you use GreaseGPT:

- **Account info** — your email address (required for sign-up) and your name (if you sign in with Google).
- **Vehicle info you enter** — make, model, year, VIN, mileage, engine, transmission, drivetrain, fuel type, service records, and any nicknames you set for your vehicles.
- **Chat messages** — everything you type to the AI mechanic (symptoms, OBD-II codes, questions).
- **Photos** — only when you attach a photo to a chat.
- **Voice recordings** — only when you use the microphone button.
- **Anonymous subscription identifier** — from RevenueCat.

## 2. What we do NOT collect

- We do **not** collect your location.
- We do **not** access your contacts, calendar, browsing history, or installed apps.
- We do **not** collect biometric data.
- We do **not** collect credit card or bank account information (Google Play Billing handles all payments).
- We do **not** collect crash logs or telemetry at this time.

## 3. Who we share your data with

We use the following third-party services strictly to make GreaseGPT work. **We do not sell your data.**

- **Anthropic (Claude)** — receives your chat text, photos, and vehicle info to generate the AI response.
- **OpenAI (Whisper)** — receives your voice recordings (if you use the mic) to transcribe them.
- **RevenueCat** — tracks your subscription across devices with an anonymous ID.
- **Google Play Billing** — processes your subscription payment.
- **Google Sign-In** — verifies your identity if you choose to sign in with Google.
- **NHTSA (U.S. Government)** — receives your VIN or make/model/year when you look up recalls or decode a VIN.

## 4. Data security

All communication with our servers uses TLS/HTTPS encryption. Passwords are hashed with bcrypt. Auth tokens use signed JWTs. We do not store credit card data at any point.

## 5. How to delete your data

Open the app → **Settings** → **DELETE MY ACCOUNT**. This immediately purges your account, vehicles, saved diagnoses, service logs, and subscription state from our servers.

## 6. Children's privacy

GreaseGPT is intended for users **13 years of age and older**. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

## 7. AI disclaimer

GreaseGPT is an **AI diagnostic assistant, not a substitute for a certified mechanic**. AI can make mistakes. Users must verify any AI-generated diagnosis with a certified mechanic before performing repairs.

## 8. Changes to this policy

If we materially change what data we collect or how we share it, we will update this page and show an in-app notice.
