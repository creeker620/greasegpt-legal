Privacy Policy for GreaseGPT

Last updated: [July 27, 2026]

GreaseGPT ("we", "us", "our", "the app") respects your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Information We Collect

When you use GreaseGPT, we may collect:

  • Account information — email address and (optionally) name, provided when you register.
  • Vehicle information — vehicle make, model, year, mileage, engine, VIN, and nickname that you enter into your Garage.
  • Diagnosis content — text messages, voice recordings, and photos you send to our AI mechanic assistant, plus the AI's replies.
  • Service records — maintenance history you optionally log.
  • Subscription data — via RevenueCat and Google Play Billing, we receive information about your subscription status (active, cancelled, expired) but never your credit card details.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To authenticate you and let you access your account.
  • To send your text, voice, and photo inputs to our AI providers (Anthropic Claude Sonnet 4.5 for chat and vision; OpenAI Whisper for voice transcription) so we can return diagnostic responses.
  • To look up vehicle information and recall campaigns via the NHTSA public API.
  • To manage your subscription status.
  • To improve the app (aggregated, anonymized metrics only).

3. Third-Party Services

We use the following third parties. Each has its own privacy policy.

  • Anthropic — privacy policy — receives chat text and photos for AI processing. Anthropic does not train on your data by default.
  • OpenAI — privacy policy — receives audio recordings for Whisper transcription. OpenAI does not train on API data by default.
  • RevenueCat — privacy policy — handles subscription lifecycle events.
  • Google Play Billing — privacy policy — processes in-app purchases.
  • NHTSA — privacy policy — public US government API used for VIN decoding and recall lookups.

4. Data Retention

  • Your account data (email, vehicles, diagnoses, service records) is retained while your account is active.
  • Voice recordings uploaded for transcription are transcribed and then deleted from our servers.
  • Photos are stored as part of your diagnosis history until you delete the diagnosis or your account.
  • We may retain purchase records longer where required by tax or legal obligations.

5. Your Rights

  • Access, edit, or delete your vehicles, diagnoses, and service records at any time from within the app.
  • Delete your entire account by emailing greasegpt.app@gmail.com (we will process deletion within 30 days).
  • Cancel your Pro subscription anytime via Google Play → Subscriptions.
  • Contact us with any privacy question at greasegpt.app@gmail.com.

6. Children's Privacy

GreaseGPT is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Security

We use industry-standard encryption in transit (TLS/HTTPS) and at rest for all user data. Passwords are hashed using bcrypt.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy occasionally. Material changes will be announced in-app and via email.

9. Contact

Questions? Email greasegpt.app@gmail.com.
